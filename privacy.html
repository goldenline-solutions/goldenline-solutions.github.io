Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Clip Machine ("the app") handles and how. Clip Machine is a self-hosted tool operated by Goldenline Solutions LLC ("we", "us"). It runs on a server we own and control. Its only user is the operator, who signs in with the operator's own TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Google accounts so the app can publish videos to those accounts. The app has no public sign-up, no website accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking.

What the app does

Clip Machine watches selected public Twitch streams (with the streamers' permission), finds highlight moments, edits them into short vertical videos with captions, and, after the operator approves each video, uploads it to the operator's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

Information the app accesses

From the platforms the operator signs in to. When the operator connects an account, the platform issues OAuth access and refresh tokens. The app uses only the permissions it needs to upload videos:

  • TikTok (user.info.basic, video.upload): the account's TikTok user ID (open_id), the granted scopes, and tokens. Used to send finished videos to the operator's TikTok drafts.
  • Instagram and Facebook (instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, business_management): the list of the operator's Facebook Pages, the ID and username of the Instagram professional account linked to a Page, and a Page access token. Used to find the operator's Instagram account and publish Reels to it.
  • YouTube (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload): tokens only. Used to upload videos to the operator's YouTube channel. The app does not read the operator's videos, subscribers, comments, watch history, or any other YouTube or Google data.

After each upload the app keeps the ID or link of the published post and any error message, so the operator can see what was posted.

From Twitch. Public stream video and audio, public chat messages (including chatters' public display names), and public viewer-made clip counts for the selected channels. Chat is held in memory to measure reactions and is not saved to disk. Stream video is kept in a rolling buffer of about two hours and then deleted automatically.

How information is used

  • Platform data and tokens are used only to publish the operator's approved videos to the operator's own accounts and to show the result of each upload. They are not used for advertising, profiling, selling, or any purpose unrelated to that.
  • A short transcript of a candidate highlight and the matching public Twitch chat lines are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to decide whether the moment is worth clipping and to write a title and caption. No TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube or Google account data or tokens are ever sent to Anthropic or to any AI model.

Storage and security

Everything is stored only on the operator's own server: tokens in a local file readable only by the app's service account, and clip details and post results in a local database. Data is sent only to the platform it belongs to (for example, a YouTube token is sent only to Google) over encrypted HTTPS connections. The review page used to approve clips is password protected and is reachable only from the operator's private network.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share information received from TikTok, Meta, or Google with anyone. It is not transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI or machine learning models. The only outside services involved are the platforms themselves (to receive uploads) and Anthropic (which receives public Twitch transcript and chat text only, as described above).

Google API Services and YouTube

Clip Machine's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; by connecting a YouTube account you are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Retention

  • Twitch stream buffer: deleted automatically after about two hours.
  • Platform tokens: kept until the operator disconnects the account or deletes them; replaced each time the account is reconnected.
  • Finished clips and post records: kept on the operator's server until the operator deletes them.

Data deletion and revoking access

To remove the app's access and delete stored data:

  1. Revoke access on each platform:
    • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
    • Facebook / Instagram: Facebook Settings › Security and login › Business integrations (or Apps and websites), then remove Clip Machine
    • TikTok: Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services, then remove Clip Machine
  2. Email goldenlinesolutionsllc0@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We will delete all tokens and account details we hold for that account from the server within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
